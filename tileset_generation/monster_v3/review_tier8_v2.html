Tier 8 v2 Summary — Magenta BG Fix

Switched 9/10 NPCs from green to magenta BG. Green BG was contaminating skin tones and natural colors.

  • Gandalf — Gray robes clean, warm skin. 46.0% final.
  • Thranduil — Warm pale elf skin, silver armor. 46.8% final.
  • Galadriel — Best improvement (+32%). White robes shine. 73.9% final.
  • Elrond — Clean dark blue robes, warm skin. 55.2% final.
  • Thorin — Ruddy dwarf skin, blue armor. 71.3% final.
  • Beorn — Tanned skin, brown leather. 50.8% final.
  • Radagast — KEPT from v1 (10/10). Brown on green works. 52.1%.
  • Eagle — Golden-brown feathers, no green tint. 42.7% final.
  • Great Elk — Natural brown fur. 52.6% final.
  • Ent — Massive improvement: 1.3% → 48.3% rembg on magenta. 54.6% final.

Key lesson: Humanoids, animals, and natural creatures should ALWAYS use magenta BG. Green BG only works for creatures with strongly non-green palettes (dark armor, purple robes, all-brown like Radagast).
