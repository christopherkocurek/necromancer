4. Proposed Luminosity Fix (Reference)

The Rule: Floors must be LIGHTER than walls. Walls must be LIGHTER than darkness. This creates instant visual hierarchy: light = passable, dark = blocked.

6. Assessment & Action Items

Review the current tiles and determine:
  1. Is the floor tile (F:1) clearly LIGHTER than the wall tile (F:56)?
  2. Is the wall tile (F:56) clearly LIGHTER than darkness (F:0)?
  3. At a glance, can you tell where the passable space is?
  4. Does the floor have a center-bright / edge-dark pattern (concave feel)?
  5. Does the wall have a top-light / bottom-dark gradient (depth)?
If the answer to 1-3 is "no", the environmental tiles need to be regenerated with:
  • Floor: ~68% average luminosity (lighter!)
  • Wall: ~30-35% average luminosity (darker than floor)
  • Darkness: ~15% luminosity (Shadow Purple #2a1a2a, not black)
  • Minimum 1.5:1 contrast ratio between floor and wall
