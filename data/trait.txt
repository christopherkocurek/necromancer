# File: trait.txt
#
# Hero trait definitions for THE NECROMANCER
# Each character selects one trait during creation.
#
# Format:
# N: ID : Name
# E: effect_id (used in code for trait logic)
# D: description (displayed to player)
#
# Traits provide a unique passive or triggered ability that
# defines the hero's archetype and playstyle.

V:1.5.0

N:0:Defiance
E:defiance
D:You fight harder against stronger foes. Gain +1 to attack
D: and damage against enemies whose native depth exceeds
D: the current floor by 3 or more.

N:1:Ambush Mastery
E:ambush_mastery
D:Your first attack against an unaware enemy deals an extra
D: damage die. Stacks with Assassination and Opening Strike.

N:2:Light of the Eldar
E:light_of_eldar
D:Your light radius is increased by 1. Undead within your
D: light radius suffer -2 to attack and evasion.

N:3:Last Stand
E:last_stand
D:When below 25% health, you gain +3 to attack, damage,
D: and evasion. Your defiance burns brightest at the end.

N:4:Fortune's Favor
E:fortunes_favor
D:Once per floor, when you would fail a saving throw or
D: resistance check, you may reroll it. Luck favors the bold.

N:5:Undying Resolve
E:undying_resolve
D:Once per game, when reduced to 0 HP, you are restored to
D: 50% health instead of dying. A light in the darkness.

N:6:Rallying Cry
E:rallying_cry
D:When you kill an enemy, all visible enemies must make a
D: Will save or lose 20 morale. Your victories inspire dread.

N:7:Song of Banishment
E:song_of_banishment
D:You begin the game with the Song of Banishment Lore ability
D: regardless of your Lore skill level.

N:8:Forge Intuition
E:forge_intuition
D:Items you pick up are automatically identified. Your
D: innate understanding of craftsmanship reveals their nature.

N:9:Shadow Step
E:shadow_step
D:Once per floor, you can teleport to any visible unaware
D: enemy's position, appearing adjacent to them. Costs 50 energy.

N:10:Steady Aim
E:steady_aim
D:When you stand still without attacking for a turn, your next
D: ranged attack gains +3 to hit and +1 critical die. Consumed
D: on attack or movement.

N:11:Oath of Enmity
E:oath_of_enmity
D:The first enemy type you kill on each floor becomes your sworn
D: foe: +2 attack, +1 damage die against that type. -1 attack
D: against all other types.

N:12:Mithril Skin
E:mithril_skin
D:You gain +1d2 innate protection, as if wearing hidden armor.
D: However, your evasion from all sources is capped at 10.
D: Requires CON 3+.

N:13:Wayfarer's Instinct
E:wayfarers_instinct
D:You detect traps within radius 3 and on entering a floor,
D: doors and stairs within radius 8 are revealed. Movement
D: noise is reduced by 2.

N:14:Nimble Striker
E:nimble_striker
D:When you move then attack in the same turn, gain +2 evasion
D: until your next turn. If you kill this way, your next move
D: costs no extra energy.

N:15:Whisper of the Valar
E:whisper_of_valar
D:Once per floor, spend 3 voice charges to reveal all enemies
D: within radius 6 for 5 turns. Revealed enemies suffer -2
D: to Will saves. Requires GRA 2+.

N:16:Blood of Numenor
E:blood_of_numenor
D:Your attacks deal +1 damage against Undead and Evil enemies.
D: You are immune to the Entranced status. However, you heal
D: 1 less from food and rest. Man race only.

N:17:Patient Stalker
E:patient_stalker
D:While in stealth with no alert enemies adjacent, gain +3
D: stealth. After 3+ consecutive stealth turns, your first
D: attack deals double damage.

N:18:Shield Brother
E:shield_brother
D:While wielding a shield: +1 melee attack and adjacent enemies
D: suffer -1 evasion. If you know Blocking, it also halves
D: incoming ranged damage.

N:19:Echoes of the Firstborn
E:echoes_firstborn
D:You begin each floor with +2 voice charges. All Lore abilities
D: cost 1 fewer voice charge (minimum 1). However, your base
D: speed is reduced by 1 tier.
