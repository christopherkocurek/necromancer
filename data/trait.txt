# File: trait.txt
#
# Hero trait definitions for THE NECROMANCER
# Each character selects one trait during creation.
#
# Format:
# N: ID : Name
# E: effect_id (used in code for trait logic)
# D: description (displayed to player)
#
# Traits provide a unique passive or triggered ability that
# defines the hero's archetype and playstyle.

V:1.5.0

N:0:Defiance
E:defiance
D:You fight harder against stronger foes. Gain +1 to attack
D: and damage against enemies whose native depth exceeds
D: the current floor by 3 or more.

N:1:Ambush Mastery
E:ambush_mastery
D:Your first attack against an unaware enemy deals an extra
D: damage die. Stacks with Assassination and Opening Strike.

N:2:Light of the Eldar
E:light_of_eldar
D:Your light radius is increased by 1. Undead within your
D: light radius suffer -2 to attack and evasion.

N:3:Last Stand
E:last_stand
D:When below 25% health, you gain +3 to attack, damage,
D: and evasion. Your defiance burns brightest at the end.

N:4:Fortune's Favor
E:fortunes_favor
D:Once per floor, when you would fail a saving throw or
D: resistance check, you may reroll it. Luck favors the bold.

N:5:Undying Resolve
E:undying_resolve
D:Once per game, when reduced to 0 HP, you are restored to
D: 50% health instead of dying. A light in the darkness.

N:6:Rallying Cry
E:rallying_cry
D:When you kill an enemy, all visible enemies must make a
D: Will save or lose 20 morale. Your victories inspire dread.

N:7:Song of Banishment
E:song_of_banishment
D:You begin the game with the Song of Banishment Lore ability
D: regardless of your Lore skill level.

N:8:Forge Intuition
E:forge_intuition
D:Items you pick up are automatically identified. Your
D: innate understanding of craftsmanship reveals their nature.

N:9:Shadow Step
E:shadow_step
D:Once per floor, you can teleport to any visible unaware
D: enemy's position, appearing adjacent to them. Costs 50 energy.
